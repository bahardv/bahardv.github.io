Hello and Welcome to My Website

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in Operations Management at the Rotman School of Management, University of Toronto, working with Prof. Sheng Liu and Prof. Nooshin Salari. I received my Ph.D. in Transportation Engineering and Management from York University, supervised by Prof. Mehdi Nourinejad and co-advised by Prof. Opher Baron and Prof. Oded Berman, as a member of the Interactive-OR Lab. Before that, I earned my M.Sc. in Air Transportation from Sharif University of Technology, where I worked on a System Dynamics model for airline expansion, and my B.Sc. in Aerospace Engineering, also from Sharif.

My research uses optimization and data-driven methods to design and evaluate healthcare and last-mile logistics systems, focusing on how emerging transportation technologies and mobility platforms change operational decisions, and when they create value. Methodologically, my work combines analytical optimization models based on continuous approximation with empirical analysis of real-world data, supported by machine learning. In last-mile logistics, I study delivery systems that use drones and autonomous vehicles. In healthcare logistics, I use large-scale ride-hailing and emergency medical services (EMS) data to design mobile automated external defibrillator (AED) networks.

I am also a recipient of the 2023 Women in Transportation WTS Toronto Chapter Award, recognizing academic achievement and contributions to advancing women in transportation.
